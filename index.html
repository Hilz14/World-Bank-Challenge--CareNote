About CareNote

Clinical validation has not been completed. Use fictional records for testing.

What it does

  • Turns the consultation into a written record, using a speech model that runs on this device.
  • Sorts the text into record sections when the clinician says a section name, such as "Medicines". This is a simple rule, not AI, and the clinician sees where everything will go before adding it.
  • Lets the clinician highlight Twi words the patient used, replay just that moment, and write the meaning. Confirmed translations build a local glossary.
  • Flags words with lower speech-model scores for clinician review. Each flagged word must be replayed and its wording or Twi meaning confirmed before adding the recording.

What it does not do

  • It does not diagnose or suggest a diagnosis.
  • It does not decide by meaning which section text belongs in. Only spoken section names move text.
  • It does not translate Twi automatically. A clinician translates every highlighted word.
  • It does not send anything to the patient's phone.
  • Review flags are not a guarantee of accuracy. A high-scoring word can still be wrong, and omitted speech cannot be flagged. Twi highlights remain clinician annotations.
  • It does not sync to DHIS2. Records can be exported as a file only.

Where data sits

  • Records are encrypted on this device (AES-GCM, key derived from the clinician's PIN with PBKDF2, 200,000 rounds) and can only be opened with that PIN.
  • Patient recordings are held in memory only and are deleted when the record is saved or discarded. They are never written to storage.
  • The app locks after 3 minutes without use. Recording, transcription and audio review count as active use; the inactivity timer restarts when they finish. Use Lock to end a session immediately and stop playback. Five wrong PINs trigger a 30 second wait. Switching clinician discards any unsaved consultation.
  • The glossary and the test log are not encrypted. Use fictional test cases and keep names and patient details out of glossary entries.
  • Readable record exports are unencrypted. Backup files keep note contents encrypted; their profile metadata, glossary and test logs remain readable.
  • A 4 digit PIN has 10,000 possible combinations and could be guessed by someone who copies this device's storage. A 6 digit PIN has 1,000,000. Use 6 to 8 digits.

Model and data

  • Speech model: Xenova/whisper-tiny.en, an ONNX conversion of OpenAI Whisper Tiny English. The converted model card declares Apache 2.0.
  • Model files reported by the loader: This excludes other app and runtime assets.
  • Automatic Twi transcription is unsupported. Accuracy on Ghanaian English and mixed speech needs direct testing; check omissions and correction time.
  • Automatic review uses the lowest decoder token probability within each word, with an initial threshold of 0.5. These scores and the threshold have not been clinically calibrated. No accuracy percentage is shown. If scores are unavailable, the clinician must replay and confirm the full recording.
